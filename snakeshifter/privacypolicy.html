Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This privacy policy describes how our game (“the App”) handles information when used by players.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

We do not collect or store any personal information directly from users. The App can be used without providing personal data such as name, email address, or account details.

2. Third‑Party Services (Advertising)

The App uses third‑party services to display advertisements, including Google AdMob.

These services may automatically collect certain information to provide ads, including:

  • Device information (such as device type and operating system)
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Android advertising ID)
  • General usage data

This information is used to serve and improve advertisements.

3. Children’s Privacy

This App is designed for children aged 7 and older.

We take children’s privacy seriously:

  • Ads shown are intended to be child‑appropriate
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children
  • Personalized advertising is disabled where required by applicable policies

Parents or guardians who believe that their child has provided personal data may contact us to request removal.

4. Internet Usage

The App requires an internet connection to:

  • Load and display rewarded video advertisements
  • Ensure ads function correctly

No additional personal data is transmitted by the App itself.

5. Data Security

We do not store user data on our own servers.
Any data handled by third‑party services (such as ad providers) is transmitted securely using industry‑standard encryption.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding data access or deletion.
For requests related to data handled by third parties, please refer to the respective provider (e.g., Google).

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time.
Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
